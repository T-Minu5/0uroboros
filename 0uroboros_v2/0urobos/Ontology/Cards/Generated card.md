# Generated card

A [[Card]] created only by effects ("Gain a Glitch", a [[Morph]] form). Never offered in the [[Draft]] and uses no market supply. 7 exist today, including Glitch, Shyte-Coin, Wave Card, Particle Card, Dit Bot, The Owl King and Razor Blade Jade.

Glitch cards have Power 0 or less and act as junk in the target's deck.
