# Discard

A [[Player]]'s used-card pile. Reshuffled into the [[Deck]] when a draw runs out.

## Arrives here
- Cards from a Node lane after [[Wave Collapse]] (unless they go to the [[Effect Bank]])
- Hand at the end of the Cycle
- [[Crypto card|Crypto cards]] after the [[Draft]]
- Purchases from the [[Market]] (unless text says otherwise)
- Expired or bumped Effect Bank cards
- Recovered cards from [[Trash]] or [[Destroyed]]
