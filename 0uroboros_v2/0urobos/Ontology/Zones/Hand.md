# Hand

Cards a [[Player]] has drawn. [[Character|Characters]], [[VP card|VP cards]] and [[Crypto card|Crypto cards]] deploy from here to Node lanes. Undeployed Crypto stays here (Wallet housing) until [[Draft]] auto-resolve.

- No maximum size.
- Cards still in hand at the end of the [[Cycle]] go to [[Discard]].
- Acted on by: Discard, Trash, Gain a card, Restore/Reset power on cards in hand.
