# Location Reward

What a [[Location]] gives when its [[Node]] resolves in [[Wave Collapse]]: its **On collapse** effects, aimed at the winner or loser. On a tie, both players receive winner rewards.

Separate from the [[Circuit Reward]], which depends on how many Locations each player won overall.
