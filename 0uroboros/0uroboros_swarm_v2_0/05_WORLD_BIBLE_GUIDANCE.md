# World Bible and Theming Guidance

- `WORLD-CORE-001` Core identity is Cyberpunk + Quantum Physics + Occult/Esoteric.
- `WORLD-CORE-002` Individual concepts ideally blend 1–2 core signals. The total world balances all three.
- `WORLD-CORE-003` Theme must not obscure concise mechanics.

## Occult pillars
Hidden knowledge, sacred geometry, alchemy/Hermetic transformation, celestial bodies, mythical beasts, Tarot/archetypes, astrology, ritual diagrams, precise symbolic linework, encoded cosmology.

## Subgenres
- `WORLD-SUB-001` Very sparse, especially Restore/healing: Solarpunk, Lunarpunk, Psychobilly.
- `WORLD-SUB-002` Moderate: Cassettepunk, Dieselpunk.
Subgenres never replace core identity.

## Obsidian
- `WORLD-OBS-001` Maintain linked worldbuilding in Obsidian-oriented structure: Characters, Factions, Locations, Cards, Mods, Technology, Occult Systems, Quantum Concepts, Terminology, Visual Motifs, Naming Conventions, Relationships.
